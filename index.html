About

Julianne Herbert is a Senior Graphic Designer based in Vancouver, BC, with over ten years of experience working with studios, agencies, and independent clients. Her work spans brand identity, editorial design, packaging, motion, and digital media.

She approaches every project with a careful eye for detail and a commitment to work that is both visually striking and conceptually grounded.

Available for freelance and collaborative projects. Get in touch at hello@julianneherbert.com.
