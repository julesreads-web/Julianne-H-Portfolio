About

Hello! I'm Julianne Herbert, a Senior Graphic Designer based in Vancouver, BC, with over 8 years of experience working across studios, agencies, and independent clients. I bring ideas to life through brand identity, editorial, packaging, motion, and digital design—shaping concepts into thoughtful, well-crafted outcomes from strategy through to execution.

My work is grounded in strong ideas, careful detail, and a belief that design should feel as good as it looks—clear, considered, and built to last.

Outside of work, I'm often lost in a good book, baking cakes for friends, or spending time outdoors.

I work on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory of the xʷməθkʷəy̓əm (Musqueam), Sḵwx̱wú7mesh (Squamish), and səlilwətaɬ (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations (a.k.a. Vancouver, BC).

I am available for freelance and collaborative projects. Get in touch at hello@julianneherbert.com
